## Specs for best quality
- **Format:** PNG with a **transparent background** (or WebP).
- **Size:** as large as you have — ideally **1500–2500 px tall**. These cover
  big portions of the screen, so small images will look soft.
- **Framing:** full body, centered, a little padding around the character.
- Keep the original look, colors and expression exactly — do not crop tightly.

After you add or replace a file, just refresh the browser.
